Why Use AI Agents?

AI agents represent the next evolution in artificial intelligence. Unlike traditional AI assistants that respond to each prompt, agents can plan multi-step workflows, execute tasks autonomously, and learn from outcomes. They're ideal for:

  • Automating repetitive coding tasks
  • Research and data synthesis
  • Building web applications without deep technical knowledge
  • Handling complex multi-step workflows
  • Software engineering and debugging
