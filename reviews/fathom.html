What is Fathom?

Fathom is an AI-powered meeting assistant that automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes your meetings. It integrates seamlessly with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams to provide instant call summaries, action items, and searchable meeting archives.

Key Features

  • Automatic Transcription: Real-time speech-to-text with high accuracy
  • Instant Summaries: AI-generated summaries within seconds of meeting end
  • Action Items: Automatically extracts tasks and assignments from conversations
  • Searchable Archive: Find any meeting detail with natural language search
  • Video Clips: Create shareable highlight clips from calls
  • CRM Integration: Syncs with Salesforce, HubSpot, and other CRM tools
  • Conversation Assistant: Ask questions about past meetings in real-time

Pros

Cons

  • Limited to Zoom, Meet, Teams

  • No on-premise option
  • Some features need premium

Verdict

Fathom is an excellent choice for professionals and teams who want to get more from their meetings without the administrative burden. The free tier is remarkably generous, making it accessible for individuals, while the team and business plans add real value for organizations needing CRM integration and collaboration features. Compared to Fireflies and Otter, Fathom strikes a good balance between features and simplicity.
