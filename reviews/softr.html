AI-Native Platform for Building Business Apps Without Code

What is Softr AI?

Softr is an AI-native platform that lets you build custom business applications, customer portals, partner platforms, and internal tools—all without writing a single line of code. Describe what you need, and Softr's AI Co-Builder instantly creates the database, application, and business logic, already connected, secure, and ready for real users.

Key Features

  • AI Co-Builder: Describe your app in plain language, and Softr generates the complete application with databases, workflows, and UI
  • 100+ Ready Templates: Pre-built templates for dashboards, customer portals, internal tools, marketplaces, and more
  • Drag-and-Drop Interface: Visual builder for customizing every aspect of your app
  • Data Source Integration: Connect to Airtable, Google Sheets, Salesforce, PostgreSQL, and other data sources
  • Member Management: Built-in authentication, roles, and permissions system
  • Real-time Sync: Automatic data synchronization across all connected sources
  • API Access: RESTful APIs for connecting to external systems
