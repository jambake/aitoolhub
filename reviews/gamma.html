What is Gamma?

Gamma is an AI-powered presentation tool that helps users create stunning slides, documents, and webpages in seconds. Instead of starting from scratch, you describe what you want and Gamma's AI generates professional, aesthetically pleasing content.

Key Features

  • One-Click Polish: AI automatically applies formatting, layouts, and styling
