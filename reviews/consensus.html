What is Consensus AI?

Consensus is an AI-powered search engine designed specifically for academic and scientific research. Unlike traditional search engines, it searches across peer-reviewed papers and provides direct answers synthesized from multiple sources, complete with citations. It's particularly valuable for researchers, students, and professionals who need to quickly find and verify information from credible academic sources.

Key Features

  • Academic Search: Searches across 200M+ peer-reviewed papers from major databases
  • AI-Powered Summaries: Provides instant summaries of research findings
  • Citation Tracking: Shows citations and links to original sources
  • Quality Indicators: Highlights paper quality, journal reputation, and author credentials
  • Multi-Topic Coverage: Covers science, medicine, business, psychology, and more
  • Research Assistant: Helps formulate research questions and find relevant literature

Who It's For

Researchers & Academics: Quickly find relevant papers and stay current with literature.
Students: Find credible sources for essays, theses, and assignments.
Professionals: Get evidence-based insights for business decisions.
Writers & Journalists: Verify facts with peer-reviewed sources.

Pricing

Consensus offers a free tier with limited searches, plus paid plans starting at $9.99/month for professional users with unlimited access and advanced features.

Our Verdict

Consensus AI is an excellent tool for anyone who needs to find credible, peer-reviewed research quickly. It's particularly useful as an alternative to Google Scholar with AI-powered summarization. The ability to get direct answers with citations makes it invaluable for academic and professional research. While it may not replace traditional database searches for deep research, it's perfect for quick literature reviews and finding credible sources.
