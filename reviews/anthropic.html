What is Anthropic?

Anthropic is an AI safety and research company founded in 2021 by former OpenAI employees. They're the creators of Claude, an AI assistant known for being helpful, harmless, and honest. Anthropic focuses on constitutional AI and RLHF to make AI systems more reliable and aligned with human values.

Key Features

  • Claude AI Assistant: Smart, nuanced conversations with strong reasoning
  • Constitutional AI: Built-in safety principles for helpful, harmless responses
  • Long Context: Claude 3.5 supports 200K token context windows
  • Computer Use: Claude can interact with computers like humans do
  • Enterprise Focus: Strong security and compliance for business use
