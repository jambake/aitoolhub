What is Seek AI?

Seek AI is an enterprise AI search platform that helps businesses find answers across their internal data sources. It connects to databases, documents, and knowledge bases to provide instant, accurate answers to complex queries.

Key Features

  • Unified Search: Search across all data sources in one place
  • Natural Language Queries: Ask questions in plain English
  • Enterprise Security: SOC 2 compliant with role-based access
  • Real-time Insights: Get instant answers from live data
  • Integration: Connects with Slack, Teams, and 50+ apps
