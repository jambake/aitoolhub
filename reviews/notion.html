What is Notion?

Notion is an all-in-one productivity workspace that combines notes, databases, project management, and wikis in one powerful platform. With Notion AI, users get AI-powered writing assistance, summarization, and automation features that supercharge productivity across teams and individuals.

Key Features

  • Notion AI: AI-powered writing, editing, and summarization
  • Databases: Flexible templates and relational databases for any use case
  • Project Management: Kanban, calendar, list, and gallery views
  • Team Wikis: Centralized knowledge bases and documentation
  • Integrations: Connect with Slack, Google Drive, Figma, and more
  • Templates: Thousands of pre-built templates for any workflow

Best For

  • Team collaboration and knowledge management
  • Project and task management
  • Content creation and writing workflows
  • Small business operations
