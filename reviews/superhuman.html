What is Superhuman?

Superhuman is an AI-powered email client built for professionals and teams who want to process email at unprecedented speed. Known as the "fastest email client ever made," it combines keyboard-first navigation with advanced AI capabilities to eliminate email drudgery.

Key Features

  • AI Write: Generate context-aware email responses instantly with AI assistance
  • AI Summarize: Get instant summaries of long email threads with one click
  • Instant Reply: Respond to emails 10x faster with AI-generated suggestions
  • Keyboard-First Design: Navigate and process emails entirely from the keyboard
  • Split Inbox: Automatically categorize emails into focused categories
  • Read States: Track email opens and get notified when recipients engage
  • Reminders: Never forget to follow up with automatic follow-up reminders
  • Undo Send: Recall sent emails within 30 seconds

Who It's For

  • Executives and founders who process high volumes of email
  • Sales professionals managing client communications
  • Remote workers seeking productivity optimization
  • Teams wanting collaborative email management

Pricing

Superhuman offers a premium pricing model:

  • Individual: $30/month
  • Team: Custom pricing with shared inboxes
  • Enterprise: Contact for pricing

Note: Premium pricing reflects the enterprise-grade features and time savings.

Our Verdict

Superhuman is a game-changer for email productivity. The keyboard-first approach, combined with powerful AI features, can save professionals 1-2 hours daily on email management. While the premium price may not suit everyone, high-volume email users will find the time savings well worth the investment.
