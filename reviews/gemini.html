Google Gemini

★★★★☆ 4.7/5.0

Google Gemini (formerly Bard) is Google's flagship AI assistant, integrated deeply across the Google ecosystem. Powered by Google's most advanced language models, Gemini offers powerful reasoning, coding, and creative capabilities.

Key Features

  • Gemini Advanced: Access to Ultra 1.0 model with 2M token context window
  • Deep Research: AI-powered comprehensive research on any topic
  • Image Generation: Create images directly via Veo 2 integration
  • Google Workspace Integration: Works seamlessly with Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Drive
  • Gemini Live: Voice conversations with Gemini on mobile
  • Think Mode: Enhanced reasoning for complex problems
  • File Analysis: Upload and analyze documents, PDFs, and images

What We Love

✓ Pros

  • Deep integration with Google ecosystem (Search, Docs, Gmail)
  • Excellent free tier with Gemini 2.0 Flash
  • Massive 2M token context on Advanced
  • Real-time information from Google Search
  • Image generation and analysis capabilities
  • Available in most languages worldwide

✗ Cons

  • Advanced features require $20/month subscription
  • Sometimes overly cautious with creative content
  • Less customizable than some alternatives

Pricing

  • Free: Gemini 2.0 Flash, basic features, standard speed
  • Gemini Advanced: $20/month - Ultra 1.0, 2M context, deep research, priority access

Best For

Google users, researchers, content creators, and anyone deeply embedded in the Google ecosystem. Particularly powerful for those who already pay for Google One.
