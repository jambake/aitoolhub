What is Cohere?

Cohere is an enterprise AI company providing large language models (LLMs) specifically designed for business applications. Unlike consumer-focused AI tools, Cohere focuses on data privacy, customization, and seamless integration with enterprise systems. Their models are available via API and can be deployed on-premises or in the cloud.

Key Features

Command Models

Fine-tuned for instruction-following and business tasks

Industry-leading text embeddings for search and RAG

Pricing

API-Based Pricing

Cohere offers pay-as-you-go pricing:

  • Command Model: ~$0.001-0.003 per 1K tokens
  • Embed Models: ~$0.0001 per 1K tokens
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for dedicated infrastructure

They also offer free API credits for testing.
