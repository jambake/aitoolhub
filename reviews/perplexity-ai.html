What is Perplexity AI?

Perplexity AI is an AI-powered search engine that provides direct, cited answers to your questions rather than just listing links. It combines the best of traditional search with large language models, pulling real-time information and presenting it in a conversational format with source citations.

Key Features

🔗 Cited Answers

Every answer includes source links, so you can verify information and dive deeper.

🔄 Real-Time Information

Access up-to-date news, research, and data with internet-connected AI models.

📊 Multiple Search Modes

Choose from Focus, Copilot, and Academic modes for different research needs.

📱 Cross-Platform

Use via web, iOS, Android, and browser extensions for seamless access.

Verdict

Perplexity AI is an excellent tool for researchers, students, and anyone who needs quick, accurate answers with source citations. It's not a replacement for creative AI tools like ChatGPT, but it's the best option for factual searches and staying informed. The free tier is solid, but the Pro ($20/month) unlocks the full potential with advanced models and unlimited searches.
