Perplexity AI

★★★★☆ 4.7/5.0

Perplexity AI is an AI-powered search engine that provides direct, cited answers to your questions rather than listing links. It combines the best of traditional search with large language model intelligence.

Key Features

  • Conversational Search: Ask follow-up questions in natural language
  • Source Citations: Every answer includes cited sources you can verify
  • Thread Support: Continue researching with related questions
  • Multiple Focus Modes: Academic, Wolfram, YouTube, and Reddit focused searches
  • Pro Search: Advanced research with better sources and larger context

What We Love

✓ Pros

  • Instant, actionable answers with citations
  • Natural conversation flow for research
  • No ad clutter or sponsored results
  • Strong free tier available
  • Great for research and fact-checking

✗ Cons

  • Less useful for simple navigational queries
