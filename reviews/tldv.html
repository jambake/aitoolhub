What is tl;dv?

tl;dv (short for "too long; didn't view") is an AI-powered meeting assistant that records, transcribes, and summarizes your Google Meet and Zoom calls. It captures every detail so you can focus on the conversation instead of taking notes. The tool integrates directly with your calendar and video conferencing apps to automatically join and record meetings.

Founded in 2020, tl;dv has grown to serve over 2 million users and has become a go-to solution for teams looking to automate their meeting documentation process. The platform uses advanced speech recognition and AI summarization to generate actionable insights from your calls.

How It Works

tl;dv works by integrating directly with your calendar and video conferencing tools. Simply connect your Google Calendar or Outlook calendar, and tl;dv will automatically detect upcoming meetings and offer to join and record them.

During the meeting, tl;dv provides a real-time transcript that you can annotate with timestamps and notes. After the meeting, the AI automatically generates a summary with key points, decisions, and action items assigned to specific team members.

The platform's AI chat feature lets you ask questions about past meetings, like "What did we decide about the marketing budget?" or "When is the launch date?" - making it easy to find information without watching entire recordings.
