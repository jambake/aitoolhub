What is Glarity?

Glarity is an AI-powered browser extension that provides instant summaries of Google search results and YouTube videos. It uses ChatGPT to generate relevant, concise summaries that save you time when researching or learning new topics.

Key Features

  • Google Search Summaries: Get AI-generated summaries of search results without clicking through multiple links
  • YouTube Video Summaries: Instantly summarize any YouTube video to find the key points
  • Cross-Platform Support: Works on Google, YouTube, Yahoo, and more
  • Customizable: Adjust summary length and style to your preferences
  • Citation Support: References are included in summaries for fact-checking
  • Language Support: Summarizes content in 30+ languages
