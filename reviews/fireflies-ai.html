What is Fireflies.ai?

Fireflies.ai is an AI-powered meeting assistant that transcribes, summarizes, and analyzes your meetings. Used by over 1 million companies, it integrates with 40+ apps including Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack, and CRMs to automate note-taking and capture action items.

Key Features

  • 95% Transcription Accuracy: Industry-leading speech-to-text in 100+ languages
  • AI Summaries: Automatically generates meeting summaries with key decisions and action items
  • 200+ AI Skills: Extract specific details, generate follow-ups, score candidates
  • Speaker Identification: Recognizes and labels different speakers automatically
  • CRM Integration: Auto-fill Salesforce, HubSpot with meeting notes and call logs
  • Project Management: Create tasks in Asana, Trello, Monday.com after meetings
  • Soundbites: Clip important moments into shareable audio snippets
  • Enterprise Security: SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR compliant

Verdict

Fireflies.ai is an excellent choice for teams that want comprehensive meeting analytics and seamless CRM integration. It's particularly strong for sales teams who need automatic call logging and follow-up creation. The 200+ AI Skills feature sets it apart from competitors, allowing you to customize exactly what data gets extracted from your meetings.

Compared to Otter.ai, Fireflies offers more integrations and better enterprise security, making it the preferred choice for larger organizations. However, Otter.ai has better real-time collaboration features. For most teams, the choice comes down to existing workflow tools and specific feature needs.
