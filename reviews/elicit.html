What is Elicit AI?

Elicit is an AI-powered research assistant designed specifically for academics and researchers. It helps users search, summarize, extract data from, and chat with over 125 million academic papers. Trusted by over 5 million researchers worldwide, Elicit streamlines the literature review process and helps researchers be 10x more evidence-based.

Key Features

  • Research Reports: Generate high-quality research briefs based on a process inspired by systematic reviews. Reports can be deeply customized - change which papers and what information is covered.
  • Systematic Literature Review: Automate screening and data extraction of systematic reviews, while partially supporting search and report generation. Researchers report up to 80% time savings.
  • Library Management: Store and organize sources as you use Elicit, making it easy to reuse them in future projects.
  • Research Alerts: Stay on top of new research without cluttering your inbox with customizable alerts.
  • 125M+ Papers: Access one of the largest academic paper databases with AI-powered search and summarization.

How It Works

Elicit uses advanced AI to understand research questions and find relevant papers even when keywords don't perfectly match. Researchers can type a research question, and Elicit searches through millions of academic papers, automatically generating structured research reports with citations in seconds.
