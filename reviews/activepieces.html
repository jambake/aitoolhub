What is Activepieces?

Activepieces is an open-source automation platform that lets you build AI agents and automations connecting apps, APIs, and data without heavy engineering. It's a modern alternative to tools like Zapier and Make, with the added benefit of being self-hostable. The platform features a visual flow builder, AI-powered automation capabilities, and a growing library of integrations.

Key Features

Visual Flow Builder

Drag-and-drop interface to create automations without writing code.

AI Agents

Build intelligent agents that can chat with your tools and take actions.

Open Source

Self-host the Community Edition for free with full control of your data.

200+ Integrations

Connect to popular apps including Slack, Gmail, Discord, and more.

Credit-Based Pricing

Pay only for what you use with flexible credit system.

Embeddable Builder

white-label the automation builder for your own SaaS product.

Verdict

Activepieces is an excellent choice for developers and small teams looking for a modern, affordable alternative to Zapier. The open-source option is particularly appealing for those who need full data control. While it may not have the extensive integration library of older platforms, it's rapidly improving and the AI agent features are ahead of the curve. Best for: developers, startups, and teams wanting to self-host their automation infrastructure.
