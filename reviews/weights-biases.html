What is Weights & Biases?

Weights & Biases (W&B) is the leading platform for machine learning experiment tracking, model visualization, and collaboration. Used by over 500,000 ML practitioners at companies like OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Toyota, W&B has become the industry standard for MLOps.

The platform allows data scientists and ML engineers to automatically log metrics, hyperparameters, code versions, and model artifacts during training, making it easy to reproduce experiments and compare results.

Key Features

Experiment Tracking: Automatically capture every detail of your ML experiments including metrics, hyperparameters, system metrics (GPU, CPU, memory), and output files. No code changes required with automatic logging for PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, and 30+ other frameworks.

Visualization Dashboard: Beautiful, interactive dashboards for comparing experiments, visualizing training curves, and analyzing model performance. Create custom charts and share insights with your team.

Sweeps: Automated hyperparameter optimization with Bayesian search, random search, and grid search. Scale sweeps across dozens of GPUs automatically.

Artifacts: Version control for your datasets, models, and files. Track lineage from raw data to deployed models.

Reports: Generate beautiful, shareable reports documenting your research. Perfect for team collaboration and stakeholder updates.

Pros

  • Free tier for individuals with full features
  • Integrates with every major ML framework
  • Excellent visualization and reporting
  • Team collaboration features are best-in-class
  • Automatic experiment logging saves hours
  • Hyperparameter sweeps are incredibly powerful

Cons

  • Team plans can get expensive
  • Steeper learning curve for beginners
  • Cloud-only (no self-hosted option)
  • Some users report API rate limiting

Pricing

Weights & Biases offers a generous free tier for individuals that includes unlimited experiments, 100GB of artifact storage, and basic collaboration features. Team plans start at $20/user/month for teams needing private projects and advanced collaboration. Enterprise plans include SSO, audit logs, and dedicated support.
