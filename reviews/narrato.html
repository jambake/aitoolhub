What is Narrato?

Narrato is an all-in-one AI content creation and workflow platform that helps teams plan, create, and optimize content at scale. It combines powerful AI writing tools with content management features, making it ideal for content marketers, agencies, and businesses.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate blog posts, social media content, ads, and more
  • Content Briefs: AI-generated briefs with keywords, structure, and references
  • SEO Tools: Built-in SEO optimization with keyword suggestions
  • Content Calendar: Plan and schedule content across channels
  • Workflow Automation: Streamline content approval and publishing
  • Team Collaboration: Multiple users, roles, and permissions
  • AI Images: Generate custom images for content
  • Plagiarism Checker: Ensure content originality

Who It's For

  • Content marketers and strategists
  • Marketing agencies managing multiple clients
  • Bloggers and content creators
  • Social media managers
  • SEO specialists
